Security

Your privacy and security are our top priority!

We offer you different options to choose from, depending on your preferences and needs. There are the solutions and regulations we have to balance your security and convenience.

Third-party authentication

This is a method that allows us to access your email content through secure and reliable third-party authorities. You need to grant us permission to use these authorities to connect your email account with our service.

Currently supports：, , and

Advantages

This method is fast, convenient, and easy to use. You do not need to forward your emails manually, and you can enjoy our service seamlessly through WhatsApp.

Disadvantages

It requires you to trust us and the third-party authorities with your email account and privacy. You also need to have an account with one of these authorities to use this method.

Forward method

This is a method that allows you to use our service by forwarding the emails that you want us to handle to a fixed email address which we provide. We will process them and send you the results through WhatsApp.

Advantages

This method is more traditional and secure. You do not need to grant us any permission to access your email account, and you can choose which emails you want us to handle.

Disadvantages

It is more tedious and time-consuming. You need to forward your emails manually, and you may miss some important emails if you forget to forward them.

end-to-end encryption & GDPR

These are some other things that we do to ensure your privacy and security. We do not store any of your private data on our server, and we try to follow WhatsApp’s encryption standards and European data regulations.

Advantages

They are meant to give you peace of mind and confidence in using our service. You can trust us with your email needs and enjoy our service without any worries.

Disadvantages

They may not be enough to satisfy some users who have higher expectations or concerns about their privacy and security. We are always open to feedback and suggestions on how to improve our service and meet your needs.
